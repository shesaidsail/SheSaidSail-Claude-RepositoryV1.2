# PURPOSE

The Founder Command Center is the surface through which Will governs the entire She Said Sail and Mare Executive operating system.

The system runs. Will governs.

This is where those two realities meet.

The Command Center is designed around one principle: Will should be able to govern the full operational intelligence layer in under 10 minutes daily and under 30 minutes weekly — from any device, in any location, without losing context.

This is not a dashboard for information consumption. It is a decision architecture.

Every element exists to surface a decision or surface the intelligence needed to make one.

---

# GOVERNING PRINCIPLES

1. Mobile-first — Will governs from his phone more often than his desk
2. Every view surfaces decisions, not just information
3. Data tiers are always visible — Tier 1 (exact), Tier 2 (operational), Tier 3 (AI guidance)
4. One-tap approvals for standard decisions
5. No information overload — surface what matters, suppress what doesn't
6. The Command Center is the only place decisions happen — no Slack approvals, no email approvals
7. Context is embedded — Will should not need to navigate elsewhere to make a decision

---

# DATA TIER VISIBILITY RULES

The Command Center enforces the Data Reliability Hierarchy at all times.

| TIER | LABEL | VISUAL TREATMENT |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Tier 1 — Exact | No label needed | Primary display — no disclaimer |
| Tier 2 — Operational Intelligence | "Updated [timestamp]" | Secondary display — staleness indicator if > 24h |
| Tier 3 — AI Guidance | "AI suggests" or "Based on recent patterns" | Clearly separated from Tier 1 — distinct visual style |

Tier 1 and Tier 3 data are never displayed in the same visual space without clear separation.

AI guidance is never styled the same as confirmed operational data.

# DECISION INTERFACE DESIGN

## One-Tap Decision Architecture

Standard decisions (approvals, lesson reviews, risk acknowledgments) present as:

```
[PROPOSAL TITLE]
[Evidence: 2–3 bullet points]
[Proposed Action: 1 sentence]
[Expected Outcome: 1 sentence]
[Confidence: 82%]

[ APPROVE ]  [ DENY ]  [ MODIFY ]  [ DEFER ]
```

Modify taps open a text field for Will to enter his version.

Defer asks: "Remind me in: 24h / 3 days / 1 week"

## Decision Notes Field

Every approve/deny/modify decision has an optional Decision Notes field.

When Will adds a Decision Note, it:
- Becomes the highest-priority context in AI future behavior for that category
- Is treated as direct operational instruction
- Never expires and never requires reconfirmation
- Cannot be modified by anyone except Will

Decision Notes are permanent institutional intelligence.

# MOBILE OPERATION STANDARDS

The Command Center is designed for mobile-first operation.

**Required mobile capabilities:**

- View Today's Decisions — fully functional on mobile
- Approve, deny, modify any decision from mobile
- Add Decision Notes from mobile
- View SEV-1 and SEV-2 risk detail from mobile
- View operational health metrics from mobile
- Access any active booking or request from mobile

**Mobile performance targets:**

- Initial load: under 3 seconds
- Decision action response: under 1 second
- Navigation between sections: under 2 seconds

**Daily mobile workflow (5 minutes):**

1. Open Today's Decisions — check for SEV-1s and critical proposals
2. Clear any immediate decisions
3. Glance at Operational Intelligence — anything anomalous?
4. Done

**Weekly mobile workflow (30 minutes — Thursday evening):**

1. Open Weekly Digest
2. Review AI-generated summary
3. Process pending lessons queue
4. Review week's risk summary
5. Process top proposals
6. Review AI quality metrics
7. Done for the week

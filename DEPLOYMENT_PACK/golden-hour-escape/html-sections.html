The experience

An hour before sunset is everything.

The Golden Hour Escape is timed around the light. You board late afternoon, once the sky has started to soften. Within the first 30 minutes on the water, that familiar shift happens: the warmth you only get when the sun is near the horizon, the kind of light that makes everyone look and feel their best.

Your crew handles everything from the moment you arrive. Bottles chilled, music low, ice filled. All you do is settle in and let the city shrink behind you. There is no agenda. The boat moves at the pace you want.

As the sun drops, the sky changes color in a way that is genuinely difficult to describe in advance. Your group has the water to yourselves. The moment is yours.

# GOLDEN HOUR ESCAPE — IMAGE DIRECTION
Version: 1.0 | Date: May 2026

---

## HERO IMAGE

**Purpose:** Set the entire emotional tone of the page in one frame.

**Direction:**
- 4–6 women on deck of a sailboat, late afternoon
- Sun is LOW in the frame or just below the horizon line
- Sky: orange, amber, deep pink gradients, not just blue
- Light falls across the group in warm horizontal rays
- Champagne glasses raised OR group relaxed and looking out at the horizon
- Crew visible but not dominant
- City skyline or open water as background
- No one is screaming. No one is looking at the camera awkwardly.
- The mood: warmth, ease, joy that is quiet, not loud

**Technical:**
- Minimum 1600px wide
- Landscape orientation
- File format: WebP or JPEG
- Max file size: 400kb (optimize before upload)
- Alt text: "Group of women celebrating on a private sailboat at golden hour, warm amber light, champagne raised, Miami coastline in the background"
- Load attribute: `loading="eager"` (above fold)

---

## EXPERIENCE SECTION IMAGE

**Purpose:** Reinforce the atmosphere of the golden hour. Intimate and specific.

**Direction:**
- Closer crop than hero -- detail shot
- Champagne flutes against the sunset backdrop, OR women talking/laughing with warm light on their faces
- No studio look. Natural movement.
- Warm grade: amber, honey, soft pink tones
- Could be a single woman looking out at the horizon

**Technical:**
- 640x480px minimum
- Aspect ratio: 4:3
- File format: WebP or JPEG
- Max file size: 150kb
- Alt text: "Women sitting on a private sailboat at sunset, warm golden light, champagne in hand, calm water"
- Load attribute: `loading="lazy"`

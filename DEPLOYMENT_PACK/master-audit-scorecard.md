## SCORING DIMENSIONS

Each page is scored on 12 dimensions, 1–10.

---

## DIMENSION DEFINITIONS

### 1. LUXURY POSITIONING (1–10)

Does the page feel premium without trying too hard?

10: Visually polished, copy restrained and confident, zero hype language
7–9: Minor issues with one of visual/copy
4–6: Inconsistency between tone and visuals, or some hype language present
1–3: Feels like a generic booking site or uses loud luxury cues

### 2. EMOTIONAL CONVERSION (1–10)

Does the page make a visitor feel something before selling to them?

10: Opens with atmosphere, earns trust emotionally, CTA feels natural
7–9: Good emotional arc but CTA arrives too early or too late
4–6: Sells before it earns, or copy is generic/flat
1–3: Purely transactional, no emotional resonance

### 3. MOBILE UX (1–10)

Is the mobile experience deliberate and comfortable?

10: All spacing correct, all CTAs work, no scroll issues, typography clear
7–9: One or two spacing or sizing issues
4–6: Noticeably cramped or awkward on mobile
1–3: Mobile is an afterthought

### 4. TRUST (1–10)

Does the page earn belief?

10: Social proof, clear detail, reassurance language, contact info visible
7–9: Minor trust gaps (missing testimonials or process clarity)
4–6: Limited social proof, vague on details
1–3: No trust signals, anonymous, no contact info

### 5. CTA CLARITY (1–10)

Is the primary action obvious and well-positioned?

10: CTA hierarchy clear, copy inviting, appears at right moments, mobile-optimized
7–9: CTA present but slightly buried or copy too generic
4–6: CTA hard to find or multiple competing CTAs
1–3: No clear conversion path

### 6. COPY (1–10)

Does the copy match brand voice and drive action?

10: Editorial, warm, precise, zero prohibited words, perfect length
7–9: Good but one section is generic or slightly over-written
4–6: Copy is functional but not premium
1–3: Robotic, salesy, or brand-damaging language present

### 7. VISUAL CONSISTENCY (1–10)

Does the page look like it belongs to She Said Sail?

10: Color, type, spacing all match system exactly
7–9: Minor inconsistency (one color off, one section crowded)
4–6: Mixed visual language, some off-brand elements
1–3: Does not feel connected to brand system

### 8. BACKEND READINESS (1–10)

Is the form wired correctly to the backend?

10: All fields present with correct names, hidden fields active, Make ready
7–9: One missing field or minor naming issue
4–6: Hidden fields missing, UTM capture absent
1–3: Form not connected to backend

### 9. ANALYTICS READINESS (1–10)

Is the page instrumented for meaningful tracking?

10: GTM active, GA4 pageview correct, form events fire, UTMs captured
7–9: Pageview tracked but form events missing
4–6: Basic pageview only
1–3: No tracking

### 10. SEO (1–10)

Is the page discoverable and properly described?

10: Title, description, OG, schema, alt text, canonical all correct
7–9: One element missing (usually schema)
4–6: Missing OG or description
1–3: Bare HTML, no metadata

### 11. PERFORMANCE (1–10)

Does the page load fast enough?

10: LCP <2.5s, CLS <0.1, all images optimized
7–9: LCP 2.5–3.5s, minor image size issues
4–6: LCP 3.5–5s, unoptimized images
1–3: LCP >5s, significant performance problems

### 12. OPERATIONAL MATURITY (1–10)

Is the page production-ready from an ops standpoint?

10: QA complete, all fields documented, backend tested, analytics verified
7–9: Minor items undocumented or untested
4–6: Notable gaps in testing or documentation
1–3: Not production-ready

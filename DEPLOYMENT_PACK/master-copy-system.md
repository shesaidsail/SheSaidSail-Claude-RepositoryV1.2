One person. Writing to another. With warmth, certainty, and no fuss.

## WRITING RULES

### Always

- Short sentences
- Serif headlines, flowing but precise
- Ground the reader in a scene or feeling before offering details
- Lead with experience, not logistics
- Active voice
- Trust the reader's intelligence
- Let white space do work

### Never

- Em dashes (prohibited brand-wide)
- "Amazing", "unforgettable", "epic", "luxury lifestyle"
- Hard-close sales language ("Book now before it's gone")
- Fake scarcity or urgency
- Hype language of any kind
- Multiple exclamation points
- "Don't hesitate to reach out"
- "VIP experience"
- "Once in a lifetime"
- "High-end vibe"
- "Premium package"
- Double-space after periods
- More than three sentences per paragraph in body copy

## HEADLINE PATTERNS

### Hero Headlines

Pattern: [Sensory anchor] + [Emotional outcome]

Examples:
"Sun on the water. Rosé in hand. Everything handled."
"Your people. The open water. Nothing else to worry about."
"She asked for a celebration. This is it."

### Section Headlines

Short. Declarative. Confident.

Examples:
"What to expect."
"How it works."
"What's included."
"The details."

Avoid:
"Discover our incredible..." (hype)
"Experience the ultimate..." (superlative)
"Book your perfect..." (pressure)

### CTA Headlines

"Ready when you are."
"Let's plan your experience."
"Tell us about your group."

## CTA COPY STANDARDS

### Primary CTA Buttons

Preferred:
- "Plan Your Day"
- "Request Your Date"
- "Inquire Now"
- "Check Availability"
- "Reserve Your Spot"

Avoid:
- "Book Now" (too transactional)
- "Click Here" (generic)
- "Submit" (cold)
- "Get Started" (startup)
- "Learn More" (weak)

### Form Submit Buttons

- "Send Your Inquiry"
- "Request Your Experience"
- "Submit Inquiry"

## REASSURANCE LANGUAGE

Use grounded, anticipatory language that removes friction before the reader feels it.

Patterns:
"We handle every detail from here."
"You'll hear from us within one business day."
"No obligation. Just a conversation."
"Everything is confirmed before you step on board."
"Your concierge handles the rest."

---

## BODY COPY PATTERNS

### Experience Descriptions

Start with atmosphere. Move to specifics.

Example:
"Rose Day Club is a full-day yacht experience built around good company, warm water, and no agenda. Departing from Fort Lauderdale, your group boards to a chilled setup of rosé, fresh fruit, and a curated soundtrack. The day unfolds at your pace. Nothing is rushed."

### Inclusions Lists

Format: short noun phrases, not sentences.

Example:
- Private yacht for your group
- Rosé and premium bar setup
- Fresh fruit and light provisions
- Floating mat and water toys
- Dedicated captain and crew
- Bluetooth speaker system
- Sunscreen and towels on board

## SOCIAL PROOF COPY

Testimonials: Use verbatim quotes. Brief preferred. No editing for hype.
Attribution: First name + occasion (e.g., "Sarah, Birthday Trip")

Format:
"The whole day was taken care of. We just showed up and it was perfect."
Sarah, Birthday Trip

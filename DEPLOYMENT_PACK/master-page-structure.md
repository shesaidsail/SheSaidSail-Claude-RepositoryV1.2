## STANDARD EXPERIENCE PAGE STRUCTURE

All experience/product pages follow this section order:

1. Hero
2. Editorial Intro
3. Experience Details
4. What's Included
5. Gallery / Atmosphere
6. Social Proof
7. Inquiry / CTA Section
8. FAQ
9. Footer CTA

---

## SECTION SPECIFICATIONS

### 1. HERO

Height: min 85vh desktop, min 70vh mobile
Background: Full-bleed image or video, dark overlay
Content:
- Label (caps, gold): e.g. "MIAMI · FORT LAUDERDALE" or experience category
- H1: Primary headline (serif, light, large)
- Sub-headline or descriptor (sans, muted, small)
- Single primary CTA button

Overlay: linear-gradient from rgba(10,35,66,0.3) to rgba(10,35,66,0.55)
Text color: #ffffff

---

### 2. EDITORIAL INTRO

Width: max 760px, centered
Padding: 96px top/bottom desktop, 64px mobile
Content:
- Gold rule (40px, 1px)
- Section label (caps, gold, 11px)
- H2 headline (serif, 36–44px)
- 2–3 paragraph body copy maximum
- No CTA in this section

Purpose: emotional landing, atmospheric description

---

### 3. EXPERIENCE DETAILS

Layout: 3 or 4-column detail cards on desktop, stacked on mobile
Background: #f9f6f0 or white
Content per card:
- Icon or number (optional)
- Label (caps, 10px, gold)
- Value (serif or sans, 20–22px)
- Optional supporting line (14px, muted)

Details covered: duration, capacity, departure location, time, pricing indication

---

### 4. WHAT'S INCLUDED

Layout: 2-column list desktop, single column mobile
Background: white
Content:
- Section label
- H2
- Bullet list (clean, no icons required)
- Optional: "Not included" section if clarifying is conversion-positive

---

### 5. GALLERY / ATMOSPHERE

Layout: masonry or grid, 3–4 images desktop, 2 column mobile
No caption required unless adding editorial value
Images: brand photography only, no stock

---

### 6. SOCIAL PROOF

Layout: 1–3 testimonials, horizontal scroll on mobile
Format: Quote text + attribution line
Background: #f9f6f0 or dark navy
Trust signals: star rating optional, press mentions if available

---

### 7. INQUIRY / CTA SECTION

Background: #0a2342 (navy) or cream
Content:
- H2 headline (calm, inviting)
- 1–2 sentence reassurance copy
- Webflow form (or inquiry form embed)
- Hidden fields: source_url, utm_*, page_name, brand, city
- Submit button follows CTA standards

---

### 8. FAQ

Layout: accordion or clean stacked Q&A
Max 6–8 questions
Tone: reassuring, not defensive
Cover: payment, cancellation, weather, what to bring, group size

---

### 9. FOOTER CTA

Background: navy or cream
Content:
- Brief editorial line
- Secondary CTA to contact or browse other experiences
- Optional: next recommended experience link

---

## NAVIGATION STANDARDS

- Sticky header on scroll: logo left, CTA right, nav center
- Nav background: transparent hero, white/navy on scroll
- Mobile: hamburger menu, full-screen overlay
- Active page indicator: gold underline

---

## SECTION TRANSITION RULES

- Adjacent same-color sections: add 1px gold rule divider
- Alternate cream/white every 2 sections where possible
- Dark sections (navy): used for CTAs and social proof only
- Never stack 2 navy sections consecutively

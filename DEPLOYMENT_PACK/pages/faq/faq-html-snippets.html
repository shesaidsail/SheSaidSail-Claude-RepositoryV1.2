The Basics

What is She Said Sail?

She Said Sail is a private yacht charter company in Miami offering curated on-water experiences for women-led celebrations. Every experience is designed around a specific feeling and occasion, not just a boat and a time slot.

How is this different from a regular yacht rental?

Most yacht rentals hand you the keys and step back. She Said Sail includes a full concierge process, curated on-board details, and experiences designed specifically for the occasions our guests are celebrating. The goal is that you never have to coordinate anything on the day.

Who typically books with She Said Sail?

Groups of women marking milestones: bachelorette parties, milestone birthdays, girls trips, relationship celebrations, and anniversary escapes. Groups range from 4 guests on an intimate charter to 22 on a full social experience.

Booking and Timing

How far in advance should I book?

Four to eight weeks is a comfortable lead time for most dates. Peak periods like spring break, Valentine's weekend, and New Year's Eve tend to book further ahead. If you have a specific date in mind, the earlier you reach out the better.

Is there a deposit to inquire or hold a date?

No deposit is required to submit a request or have the initial concierge conversation. A deposit is only required once you are ready to confirm the date, and the terms are discussed clearly before anything is charged.

What happens after I submit the Request to Book form?

A concierge reaches out within 24 hours to discuss your date, group size, and any preferences. From there you will receive a clear proposal and have time to ask questions before anything is confirmed.

The Experience

What is included in the charter?

Every experience includes a private yacht, professional captain and crew, welcome champagne or rosé service, curated music, and access to Miami's waterways. Specific inclusions vary by experience. The full list is on each experience page.

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

In most cases, yes. The concierge will confirm what is permitted for your specific experience and can also arrange additional catering or beverage service if you prefer to have everything handled.

Can we choose the music?

Yes. Share a playlist in advance and the crew will handle it. If you prefer not to, they will curate something appropriate for your occasion. Either way, the music is one less thing to think about on the day.

Can the experience be customized?

Yes, within reason. Common additions include florals, custom signage, specific beverages, or a particular timing for golden hour. The concierge will let you know what is possible for your date and experience.

Logistics

Where does the charter depart from?

Departure location is confirmed during the booking process and depends on the experience and vessel. Most departures are from marinas in Miami or Fort Lauderdale. The concierge will send full location details before your day.

How long do the experiences run?

Duration varies by experience. Most run between 3 and 6 hours. Check the quick facts on each experience page, or ask your concierge when you reach out.

Is gratuity included?

Gratuity is not included in the charter price. Industry standard is 15 to 20 percent, paid directly to the captain and crew. The concierge will note this clearly in your confirmation so there are no surprises.

Weather

What happens if the weather is bad?

Miami's weather is generally predictable and charter-friendly. If conditions on your date are not suitable for a safe and enjoyable experience, the concierge will work with you to find an alternative date. Guest safety and comfort come first.

Can I see a weather policy before I book?

Yes. The full weather and rescheduling approach is covered in the confirmation documents shared before your deposit. The concierge is also happy to walk through it during the initial conversation.

Group Size

What is the minimum and maximum group size?

Minimums vary by experience, and maximums range from 12 guests on intimate charters to 22 on the Pink Palm Club. If you are unsure, the concierge will help match your group size to the right experience.

Can I book for a smaller group than the experience minimum?

Some flexibility exists depending on the date and availability. Reach out and the concierge will find the best option for your group.

After Your Experience

How do I leave a review or share feedback?

After your experience, you will receive a brief follow-up from the team. Sharing your experience publicly on Google or through word of mouth is genuinely appreciated. Real guest stories are the most meaningful thing She Said Sail has.

Can I book again for a different occasion?

Returning guests are always welcome. The concierge keeps notes from your previous experience so the next one starts from a better place.

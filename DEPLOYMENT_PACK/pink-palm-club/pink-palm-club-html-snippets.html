The experience

Not a boat trip. A full afternoon designed around you.

Pink Palm Club is She Said Sail's signature group experience. From the moment you step aboard, the day is already in motion. Music, food, a captain who knows exactly how to read the afternoon, and a deck full of the people you actually want around you.

We handle the logistics. You bring the group. The water takes care of the rest.

This experience works for bachelorettes, birthdays, girls trips, and any occasion that deserves a day that feels different from all the ones before it.

What is included

Everything that makes the day run smoothly.

  • Private yacht charter for your group only
  • Licensed captain and one crew member
  • Curated playlist and Bluetooth audio system
  • Full cooler with ice, mixers, and soft drinks
  • Snorkel gear for the group
  • Towels, sunscreen, and deck seating for everyone
  • Anchorage at a private sandbar (weather permitting)
  • Boarding assistance and welcome onboard
  • Dedicated pre-charter concierge support

# How It Works Section

Purpose: remove the fear of a long, complicated sales process. Place this on the homepage and on the request to book page.

## Headline

How She Said Sail Works

## Steps

1. Tell us about your group
   Share your date, occasion, guest count, and what kind of day you want.

2. We recommend the right yacht
   Our concierge team narrows the options and points you toward the best fit.

3. Reserve your date
   Once availability is confirmed, we send the next steps to secure your charter.

4. We handle the details
   From timing to add ons, our team helps make the day feel effortless.

5. Show up and enjoy it
   Your group arrives ready for a private yacht experience built around your celebration.

- Use an icon list or a row of step columns, one column per step
- Keep the numbers visible
- Headline as a heading widget, steps as text or icon box widgets

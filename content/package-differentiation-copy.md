# Package Differentiation Copy

Purpose: let a first time visitor self select quickly. Under each experience name, add one clear, buyer friendly sentence so visitors choose instead of browse.

Brand voice note: the brand guidelines lean away from the word "package" in client facing copy and toward words like curated, designed, and handled. The one line descriptions below avoid the word where possible. Use these as the differentiator line directly under each name.

## Rosé

One line description:
> Best for smaller celebrations, first time yacht days, and groups who want a beautiful private experience without overcomplicating the plan.

## Sunset Social

One line description:
> Best for golden hour celebrations, birthdays, and groups who want a more elevated evening on the water.

## Pink Palm

One line description:
> Best for bachelorettes and girls trips that want the full She Said Sail experience with more polish, planning, and photo ready details.

Supporting detail from the real Pink Palm copy (optional secondary line):
> Bold, electric, Miami. The most booked experience, built for groups who came to celebrate fully.

## Monaco Social

One line description:
> Best for premium celebrations, larger budgets, and groups who want the most elevated version of the day handled from start to finish.

Supporting detail from the real Monaco Social copy (optional secondary line):
> Champagne led and Riviera inspired. Everything is already in place before you arrive.

## Every experience includes

Use this line near the differentiators so the value is clear:
> Every experience includes the yacht, captain, crew, planning, styling, setup, premium beverages, fresh florals, charcuterie, and concierge service. Everything is already handled. You just show up.

## Placement

Place each one line description directly under its name on the pricing page and on any vessel card that lists the four experiences. Keep them short. One sentence each.

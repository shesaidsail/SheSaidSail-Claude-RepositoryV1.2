# Weather and Rescheduling Reassurance Block

Purpose: remove the biggest pre booking anxiety after "is this real?" which is "what if something goes wrong with the weather?" This block stays calm and premium and does not invent a refund policy or guarantee anything not supported by the legal documents.

## Approved copy (use exactly as written)

> Weather happens. If conditions are not suitable for your charter, our team will walk you through the available rescheduling options and coordinate next steps with the vessel provider.

## Where to place it

- Near pricing tables
- On the booking and request to book page
- Inside or directly above the FAQ
- Near any deposit or reserve action

## Rules

- Do not overpromise
- Do not invent a refund policy
- Do not guarantee anything not supported by legal documents
- Keep it calm and premium

## Optional short label version (for tight spaces)

> Weather happens. If conditions are not suitable, we will walk you through your rescheduling options.

## Elementor placement note

Add as a small text or icon box widget directly beneath pricing and near the reserve action. Keep typography consistent with surrounding body copy. Do not place it inside the testimonial or photo sections.

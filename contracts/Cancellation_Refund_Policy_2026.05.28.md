## 1. PAYMENT SCHEDULE

**1.1 Deposit.** A non-refundable deposit equal to fifty percent (50%) of the total booking price is required at the time of booking to confirm a reservation. No reservation shall be deemed confirmed until the applicable deposit has been received and processed.

**1.2 Remaining Balance.** The remaining fifty percent (50%) balance of the total booking price is due no later than seventy-two (72) hours prior to the scheduled charter date and time. Failure to remit the remaining balance within this timeframe may result in automatic cancellation of the reservation and forfeiture of the deposit, at the Company's sole discretion.

**1.3 Payment Processing.** All payments are processed through Stripe. By submitting payment, you agree to Stripe's terms and conditions. The Company acts solely as a payment processing intermediary on behalf of the applicable Operator and is not the provider of charter services.

---

## 2. CHARTERER-INITIATED CANCELLATIONS

**2.1 General Rule.** All deposits are non-refundable. If you cancel a confirmed reservation for any reason, the fifty percent (50%) deposit paid at the time of booking is forfeited and will not be returned under any circumstances.

**2.2 Cancellation More Than 72 Hours Before Charter.** If you cancel a confirmed reservation more than seventy-two (72) hours prior to the scheduled charter date and time, you will forfeit the deposit. Any balance paid beyond the deposit will be refunded to the original payment method within seven (7) to ten (10) business days.

**2.3 Cancellation Within 72 Hours of Charter.** If you cancel a confirmed reservation within seventy-two (72) hours of the scheduled charter date and time, the full amount paid — including both the deposit and any remaining balance — is non-refundable. No exceptions will be made for personal emergencies, travel delays, or changes in plans.

**2.4 No-Show.** If you and your party fail to appear at the confirmed embarkation location at the scheduled departure time without prior notice, the booking will be treated as a same-day cancellation. No refund of any amount paid will be issued.

**2.5 Partial Cancellations.** If the size of your group decreases after booking confirmation, the booking price will not be adjusted downward and no partial refunds will be issued for unused capacity.

---

## 3. OPERATOR-INITIATED CANCELLATIONS

**3.1 Vessel Unavailability.** In the event that a confirmed reservation cannot be fulfilled due to vessel unavailability caused by the Operator, including mechanical failure, maintenance requirements, or other operator-side circumstances, the Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to arrange placement on a comparable replacement vessel, subject to availability.

**3.2 No Suitable Replacement.** If a suitable replacement vessel cannot be arranged, or if you decline the proposed replacement, you will be entitled to a full refund of all amounts paid to the Company through the Platform for the affected booking. Such refund will be processed within seven (7) to ten (10) business days to the original payment method.

**3.3 Sole Remedy.** The refund described in Section 3.2 constitutes your sole and exclusive remedy with respect to an Operator-initiated cancellation. The Company shall have no further liability to you, including but not limited to any incidental, consequential, or other damages.

## 4. WEATHER AND SAFETY CANCELLATIONS

**4.1 Company Discretion.** The Company reserves the right, in its sole and absolute discretion, to cancel or postpone any reservation at any time due to adverse weather conditions, unsafe sea conditions, Coast Guard advisories, port closures, safety concerns, or any other circumstances the Company deems to pose a risk to the safety of passengers, crew, or vessel.

**4.2 Refund on Company-Initiated Cancellation.** In the event of a cancellation initiated by the Company under Section 4.1, you will be entitled to a full refund of all amounts paid through the Platform for the affected booking, or, at the Company's election, a rebooking credit of equal value applicable to a future reservation, subject to availability.

**4.3 Captain's Authority.** The captain of any vessel has ultimate authority over all safety decisions, including but not limited to departure, routing, anchoring, and early return. A captain's decision to modify or terminate a charter due to safety conditions does not constitute a cancellation by the Company or the Operator and may not entitle you to a refund, depending on the circumstances and at the Company's sole discretion.

**4.4 No Liability for Weather.** The Company shall not be liable for any damages, losses, expenses, or inconveniences arising from weather-related cancellations, modifications, or delays, including but not limited to travel costs, accommodation costs, or other related expenses.

## 6. RESCHEDULING AND REBOOKING

**6.1 Charterer-Requested Changes.** Requests to reschedule a confirmed reservation are subject to vessel and Operator availability and must be submitted to the Company at least seventy-two (72) hours prior to the original charter date. The Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to accommodate rescheduling requests but cannot guarantee availability. Rescheduling is not guaranteed and is granted at the Company's sole discretion.

**6.2 Rescheduling Fee.** A rescheduling fee of up to fifteen percent (15%) of the total booking price may be assessed for Charterer-requested date changes, depending on the circumstances and availability.

**6.3 No Rescheduling Within 72 Hours.** Rescheduling requests submitted within seventy-two (72) hours of the original charter date will not be accommodated. The full booking amount is forfeited in such cases.

**7.1 Timing.** Approved refunds will be processed within seven (7) to ten (10) business days of the cancellation being confirmed by the Company. Refunds are returned to the original payment method used at the time of booking.

**7.2 Stripe Processing.** Refunds are issued through Stripe, the Company's payment processor. Processing times may vary depending on your financial institution. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by your bank or card issuer.

**7.3 Credit Card Chargebacks.** Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank for a charge that is subject to this Policy, without first contacting the Company and following the refund process outlined herein, constitutes a breach of these Terms and may result in termination of your account and forfeiture of any amounts paid.

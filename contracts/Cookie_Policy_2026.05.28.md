## 1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites function correctly, to improve user experience, and to provide reporting information to website operators and third parties.

Cookies set by the Company are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by third parties other than the Company — such as analytics providers and advertising networks — are called "third-party cookies." Both types are addressed in this Policy.

In addition to cookies, we also use related technologies including:

- **Pixel tags / web beacons:** Transparent image files (often 1x1 pixels) embedded in web pages or emails that track user behavior and measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.
- **Local storage objects:** Data stored locally on your browser, similar to cookies but with greater capacity.
- **Session storage:** Temporary data stored for the duration of a browser session and deleted when you close your browser.

## 2. HOW WE USE TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

We and our third-party partners use Tracking Technologies for the following purposes:

**(a) Platform Operation and Security.** To enable core Platform functionality, including account authentication, session management, fraud prevention, and security monitoring.

**(b) Performance and Analytics.** To collect data about how Visitors use the Platform — which pages are viewed, how long Visitors stay, which features are used, and whether error messages occur — so we can improve the Platform's performance and user experience.

**(c) Advertising and Marketing.** To deliver advertisements relevant to your interests on the Platform and on third-party websites and platforms; to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns; and to limit the number of times you see an advertisement.

**(d) Personalization.** To remember your preferences and settings (such as language, region, and display choices) and to provide a more personalized experience on the Platform.

**(e) Booking and Transaction Functionality.** To maintain booking session state, preserve form inputs, and facilitate payment processing through our third-party payment processor, Stripe.

## 3. CATEGORIES OF COOKIES WE USE

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Platform to function and cannot be disabled. They do not collect personal information for advertising purposes. Without these cookies, services you have requested — such as account login, booking session management, and secure payment processing — cannot be provided.

| Cookie / Technology | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|

### 3.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect anonymous information about how Visitors use the Platform. The data collected is aggregated and used to improve Platform performance and content. We use the following analytics providers:

**Google Analytics**
We use Google Analytics to understand how Visitors interact with the Platform, including pages visited, traffic sources, and session duration. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information and transmits it to Google's servers in the United States. Google may use this data in accordance with its own privacy policy. For more information, visit: www.google.com/analytics/terms. To opt out of Google Analytics across all websites, visit: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

**Meta Pixel (Facebook Pixel)**
We use the Meta Pixel, a piece of code provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta"), to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Meta's platforms (including Facebook and Instagram), to build advertising audiences, and to retarget Visitors who have previously interacted with the Platform. The Meta Pixel collects information including pages viewed, actions taken on the Platform, your IP address, browser type, and whether you have a Facebook or Instagram account. This data is transmitted to Meta and processed in accordance with Meta's Data Policy, available at www.facebook.com/policy.php. For more information about how Meta uses this data for advertising, visit www.facebook.com/legal/business_terms. To manage your Meta advertising preferences, visit www.facebook.com/settings?tab=ads.

| Cookie / Technology | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| _ga, _gid, _gat | Google Analytics | Website usage analytics | 2 years / 24 hours / 1 minute |
| _fbp, _fbc | Meta Pixel | Advertising measurement and retargeting | 90 days |

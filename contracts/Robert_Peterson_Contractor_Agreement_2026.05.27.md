INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of May 27, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between She Said Sail LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("Company"), and Robert Peterson, an individual ("Contractor"). Company and Contractor are each referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Company desires to engage Contractor to provide software development and technology services in connection with Company's internal operations portal and related technology infrastructure; and

WHEREAS, Contractor desires to provide such services to Company, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement; and

WHEREAS, Contractor has commenced certain initial services and Company has paid an initial retainer of $450.00 prior to the execution of this Agreement, which payment shall be governed by the terms hereof;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. Services.

**1.1 Engagement.** Company hereby engages Contractor, and Contractor hereby agrees to provide to Company, software development, codebase audit, deployment, and technology maintenance services as more particularly described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW") executed by the Parties from time to time and incorporated herein by reference. The initial scope of services is set forth in Exhibit A attached hereto.

**1.2 Performance Standard.** Contractor shall perform all services in a professional, workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards for senior software development, using best practices for security, code quality, and maintainability.

**1.3 Tools and Equipment.** Unless otherwise specified in an SOW, Contractor shall furnish all tools, software, equipment, and resources necessary to perform the services. Contractor shall not charge Company for any third-party subscriptions or software licenses without prior written approval.

**1.4 Subcontracting.** Contractor shall not subcontract, delegate, or otherwise transfer any services or obligations under this Agreement to any third party without Company's prior written consent.

---

## 2. Independent Contractor Status.

**2.1 Independent Contractor.** Contractor is, and at all times shall remain, an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates or shall be deemed to create any partnership, joint venture, agency, franchise, fiduciary, employment, or similar relationship between the Parties. Contractor has no authority to bind Company, incur obligations on behalf of Company, or make representations or commitments in Company's name.

**2.2 No Employee Benefits.** Contractor shall not be entitled to any employee benefits, including health insurance, retirement plans, paid leave, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance. Contractor is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes, including self-employment taxes, arising from compensation received under this Agreement.

**2.3 Method of Performance.** Contractor shall have the right to control the manner and means by which the services are performed, subject to the requirements of each SOW and this Agreement. Company's right is limited to specifying the desired results.

---

## 3. Compensation.

**3.1 Hourly Rate.** Company shall pay Contractor at the rate of $45.00 per hour for all services performed under this Agreement, unless a different rate is expressly specified in an applicable SOW.

**3.2 Initial Retainer.** Company has paid Contractor an initial retainer of $450.00 (the "Initial Retainer") prior to the Effective Date, which shall be applied as prepayment against the first ten (10) hours of services rendered under this Agreement. If Contractor performs fewer than ten (10) hours of services and this Agreement is terminated, Contractor shall refund to Company the unused portion of the Initial Retainer within fifteen (15) days of termination.

**3.3 Invoicing.** Contractor shall submit itemized invoices to Company on a bi-weekly basis (or as otherwise agreed in an SOW), detailing the hours worked, tasks completed, and any pre-approved expenses. Invoices shall be submitted to hello@shesaidsail.com.

**3.4 Payment.** Company shall pay undisputed invoices within fifteen (15) days of receipt of a correct, itemized invoice.

**3.5 Expenses.** Company shall reimburse Contractor for reasonable, pre-approved, and documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing the services. Contractor shall not incur any expense exceeding $50.00 without Company's prior written approval.

---

## 4. Intellectual Property.

**4.1 Work Made for Hire.** All work product, deliverables, code, software, documentation, configurations, designs, and other materials created, developed, or produced by Contractor in connection with the services (collectively, "Work Product") shall be considered "work made for hire" for Company within the meaning of the U.S. Copyright Act, and all right, title, and interest in and to all Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein, shall be owned exclusively by Company from the moment of creation.

**4.2 Assignment.** To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as "work made for hire" under applicable law, Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product, including all intellectual property rights, moral rights, and all claims and causes of action related thereto, without further consideration.

**4.3 Pre-Existing Materials.** If Contractor incorporates any pre-existing materials, tools, or intellectual property owned by Contractor ("Pre-Existing Materials") into the Work Product, Contractor hereby grants Company a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, and sublicense such Pre-Existing Materials as necessary to use and exploit the Work Product. Contractor shall disclose any Pre-Existing Materials incorporated into Work Product at the time of delivery.

**4.4 Assistance.** Contractor shall, upon Company's request and at Company's expense, execute such documents and take such actions as Company may reasonably request to perfect, confirm, and enforce Company's ownership rights in the Work Product.

---

## 5. Confidentiality.

**5.1 Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public, proprietary, or confidential information of Company disclosed to Contractor in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to: source code, API keys, environment variables, access credentials, database schemas, customer data, client lists, booking records, financial information, business plans, marketing strategies, pricing, and any other information that a reasonable person would consider confidential.

**5.2 Obligations.** Contractor shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely to perform the services under this Agreement; (c) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without Company's prior written consent; and (d) implement reasonable security measures to protect Confidential Information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

**5.3 Credentials and System Access.** Without limiting Section 5.2, Contractor shall: (a) never store, transmit, or share access credentials (including .env files, API keys, and passwords) in plain text via email, text message, or any unencrypted channel; (b) use only Company-approved secure methods for receiving credentials; (c) promptly notify Company of any actual or suspected unauthorized access to Company systems; and (d) immediately return or destroy all credentials upon termination of this Agreement.

**5.4 Survival.** Contractor's obligations under this Section 5 shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement indefinitely with respect to trade secrets, and for a period of five (5) years from termination with respect to all other Confidential Information.

## 6. Non-Solicitation.

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of one (1) year following its termination or expiration, Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly: (a) solicit, recruit, or hire any employee, contractor, or consultant of Company; or (b) solicit or attempt to obtain business from any customer, client, or business partner of Company with whom Contractor had contact in connection with the services.

## 7. Representations and Warranties.

Contractor represents, warrants, and covenants that: (a) Contractor has the full right, power, and authority to enter into this Agreement and perform the services; (b) the services will be performed in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations; (c) the Work Product will not infringe upon or misappropriate any intellectual property rights of any third party; (d) Contractor is not subject to any agreement, obligation, or restriction that would conflict with or prevent Contractor from performing the services; and (e) all deliverables will be free from material defects in workmanship.

---

## 8. Term and Termination.

**8.1 Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with this Section 8.

**8.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, upon seven (7) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

**8.3 Termination for Cause.** Company may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if Contractor: (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within five (5) days of written notice; (b) misappropriates Confidential Information; (c) engages in fraud, misconduct, or criminal activity; or (d) fails to perform the services in a satisfactory manner after receiving written notice of deficiencies.

**8.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination: (a) Contractor shall promptly deliver to Company all Work Product, work in progress, and Company property in Contractor's possession; (b) Contractor shall immediately return or destroy all Confidential Information and credentials; (c) Company shall pay Contractor for all services satisfactorily rendered through the effective date of termination; and (d) Sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, and 11 shall survive termination.

## EXHIBIT A — INITIAL STATEMENT OF WORK

**SOW No.:** 001
**Effective Date:** May 27, 2026

**Services:** Contractor shall provide the following software development and technology services:

1. **Portal Deployment Audit** — Review, debug, and resolve all deployment blockers for the She Said Sail internal operations portal ("sss-portal"), a Next.js 14 application deployed on Vercel.

2. **Authentication Implementation** — Assist with configuring and validating NextAuth v4 with Airtable-backed user authentication, including environment variable setup and testing.

3. **Codebase Review** — Audit the sss-portal codebase for bugs, TypeScript errors, security issues, and deployment readiness.

4. **Vercel Deployment** — Ensure successful production deployment on Vercel including correct environment variable configuration.

5. **Ongoing Maintenance** — Provide ongoing bug fixes, feature development, and optimization as requested by Company, billed hourly at $45.00/hour.

**Estimated Hours:** Open-ended; billed hourly.

**Initial Payment:** $450.00 retainer (pre-paid; applies against first 10 hours).

**Deliverables:** Working portal deployment; resolved TypeScript and build errors; documentation of all environment variables and system configuration.

**Access Requirements:** Contractor will require read/write access to the Company GitHub repository and secure access to environment variables. All credentials shall be shared via Company-approved secure method only (never plain email or SMS).

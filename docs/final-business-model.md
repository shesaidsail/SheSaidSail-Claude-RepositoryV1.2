# She Said Sail: Final Business Model

One answer, built on the real data collected (Gale costs, Airtable, website pricing, ACF, competitor research). Goal: the easiest luxury yacht celebration in Miami to say yes to, while being highly profitable and scalable. Not maximum margin. Not lowest price. No production data changed; this is the approval ready plan.

## Phase 3 — Fleet design (assuming access to smaller yachts)

The best economics in the data are the smaller, cheaper to source boats. Smaller yachts (about 65 to 80ft) still seat 12 for a day charter, which is the bachelorette and birthday sweet spot, and they source for $4,500 to $6,000. They are the growth engine. Add two to three.

| Volume target | Volume fleet | Core fleet | Prestige fleet | Booking mix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 per month | 4 to 5 boats (Carpe Diem, Freedom, Vasiliki, Tranquility IV, plus 1 small) | Mirracle, Gatsby | Another One, Carpe Diem Premium | 14 / 7 / 4 |
| 50 per month | 6 to 7 boats (add 2 small 65 to 75ft) | Mirracle, Gatsby | Another One, Carpe Diem Premium, plus GTX 80 if renegotiated | 28 / 15 / 7 |
| 100 per month | 8 to 10 boats (add 3 to 4 small) | Mirracle, Gatsby, plus 1 to 2 mid | 2 to 3 flagships | 55 / 30 / 15 |

Each yacht handles roughly 8 to 12 bookings per month at most, so 100 per month needs about 10 to 12 active vessels, most in the Volume tier.

## Phase 4 — What business are we in

Not yacht size. Not lowest price. She Said Sail is in the produced celebration business: the easiest, fully handled luxury yacht day for bachelorettes, birthdays, girls trips, and private celebrations. The product is the day, not the boat. Compete on all inclusive simplicity plus concierge production, where the only competitors are DIY self planning and a la carte charters that hand the client a logistics project. This maximizes conversion (no decision fatigue, one clear price), referrals (celebration groups refer), reviews (a delighted group posts), and brand value (a category leader, not a broker).

## Phase 6 — Inclusions, optimized for profit without hurting conversion

Principle: include whatever creates the handled, easy yes feeling and drives reviews. Make incremental luxuries upsells.

| Item | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Vessel, captain, crew, fuel | Included | Already in Boat Cost from Gale |
| Champagne and signature cocktail | Included | Core brand promise, drives the experience and reviews |
| Charcuterie and florals and base styling | Included | Defines the produced day |
| Concierge planning | Included | The differentiator, already $550 |
| Premium champagne (Veuve) and caviar | Tier based | Monaco and Pink Palm only |
| Elevated decor | Tier based | Pink Palm and Monaco |
| Gift bags | Upsell, included only on Monaco | $1,200 at 12 guests breaks entry margin, keep as the $195 per guest add on elsewhere |
| Transportation | Small included credit | Cheap goodwill, delighter |
| DJ, photographer, private chef, live music | Upsells | High margin, raise average booking value, already catalogued |

This keeps a clean, confident base price (better conversion) while the upsells carry extra margin and lift average booking value.

## Phase 7 — The one recommendation

1. The ideal fleet: Keep six (Carpe Diem, Freedom, Vasiliki, Tranquility IV as Volume, Mirracle and Gatsby as Core). Add two to three small 65 to 75ft boats sourced at $4,500 to $6,000 as Volume. Keep two flagships (Another One, Carpe Diem Premium) as Prestige. Renegotiate Sugaree and GTX 80, and if Gale will not move, replace them with better sourced 72 to 80ft vessels.
2. The ideal pricing: Volume hold at current ($9,900 to $11,500, 20 to 25%). Core up modestly (Mirracle $15,500, Gatsby $18,500, about 27 to 30%). Prestige flagships up (Carpe Diem Premium $38,500, Another One $44,000, about 36 to 37%). Do not raise Sugaree or GTX 80 into the market vacuum.
3. The ideal package structure: keep the four experiences (Rosé Day Club, Golden Hour Escape, Pink Palm Club, Monaco Social) with the existing plus 6, 12, 22% premiums. They self select cleanly and the premiums are high margin.
4. The ideal inclusions: per Phase 6. Clean all inclusive base, gift bags and premium champagne and entertainment as upsells, gift bags included only on Monaco.
5. The ideal booking mix: 55 Volume, 30 Core, 15 Prestige per 100 bookings.
6. The ideal supplier strategy: keep the well priced Gale boats, renegotiate the two overpriced ones, and diversify sourcing by adding small value vessels. Never let an input cost exceed the comparable market retail.
7. The expected economics (blended contribution per booking about $3,000 Volume, $5,800 Core, $14,800 Prestige):

| Bookings per month | Mix V/C/P | Contribution | Less $10k overhead | Monthly profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 14 / 7 / 4 | about $142,000 | minus $10,000 | about $132,000 |
| 50 | 28 / 15 / 7 | about $274,000 | minus $10,000 | about $264,000 |
| 100 | 55 / 30 / 15 | about $562,000 | minus $10,000 | about $552,000 |

Prestige volume is demand capped, so treat the prestige counts as a ceiling, not a given. Even if prestige under fills, the Volume and Core tiers alone clear the overhead many times over. Break even remains about 2 to 3 bookings per month.

## The bottom line

Lead with a cheap to source, competitively priced Volume fleet that wins the easy yes and floods reviews and referrals. Carry profit with two Core boats priced to the market ceiling. Crown the brand with two flagships at true luxury prices and low volume. Fix the two broken supplier deals at the source rather than with price. Keep the base all inclusive and clean, and make the luxuries upsells. That is the easiest luxury yacht celebration in Miami to say yes to, and a highly profitable, scalable company.

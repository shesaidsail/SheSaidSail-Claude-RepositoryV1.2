# Fleet Portfolio Strategy

Treat the fleet as a portfolio, not as one margin rule. Classify by cost to source and demand, set a different margin target per tier, and choose the booking mix that maximizes total profit, reviews, referrals, and market share. Based on actual Gale costs. No production pricing changed. Tiers and targets are tagged in the Sandbox Yacht Broker Rates table (Fleet Tier, Target Margin).

## Classification

| Tier | Margin target | Yachts | Logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volume Fleet | 20 to 25% | Carpe Diem, Freedom, Vasiliki, Tranquility IV | Cheapest to source ($4,500 to $5,400 at 4hr), best natural markup. Price competitively, win the easy yes, generate reviews and referrals. |
| Core Profit Fleet | 30 to 35% | Mirracle, Gatsby | Mid cost ($7,650 to $9,900), strong recognizable vessels. The profit backbone. |
| Prestige Fleet | 35 to 40% | Sugaree, GTX 80, Carpe Diem Premium, Another One | Most expensive to source ($11,250 to $22,500), newest and largest. Price for margin and halo, accept lower volume. |

Compass is unclassified pending its Gale rate.

## 100 booking model

### Mix options

| Mix | Volume | Core | Prestige | Total contribution | Reviews and referrals | Market share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All Volume | 100 | 0 | 0 | about $290,000 | highest | highest |
| All Prestige | 0 | 0 | 100 | about $1,125,000 on paper | lowest | lowest, and demand cannot fill it |
| Recommended | 55 | 30 | 15 | about $532,250 | high | high |

All Prestige is a paper number. Prestige demand cannot supply 100 bookings, and a prestige only book would starve the review, referral, and market share engine. All Volume maximizes the flywheel but leaves most profit on the table. The recommended mix captures most of the flywheel and far more profit.

### Recommended 100 booking mix

| Tier | Bookings | Contribution each | Tier contribution | Share of profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volume Fleet | 55 | $2,900 | $159,500 | 30% |
| Core Profit Fleet | 30 | $6,800 | $204,000 | 38% |
| Prestige Fleet | 15 | $11,250 | $168,750 | 32% |
| Total | 100 | | $532,250 | 100% |

Blended contribution about $5,320 per booking. Versus an all volume book at the same 100 count, this is about 1.8 times the profit, while keeping 85 of 100 bookings in the review and referral generating tiers.

### Why this mix maximizes the four goals

- Profit: Core and Prestige are only 45 of 100 bookings but deliver about 70 percent of contribution ($372,750). They carry the profit so Volume does not have to.
- Reviews: 55 Volume bookings means the most customers, which means the most reviews. Review velocity comes from booking count, and Volume supplies it.
- Referrals: Volume and Core are the bachelorette, birthday, and girls trip groups that refer heavily. 85 of 100 bookings sit there.
- Market share: the Volume fleet, priced competitively at 20 to 25 percent, wins the comparison shopper and seeds the market, while Prestige sets the brand ceiling.

## Recommended fleet strategy

1. Keep the Volume fleet (Carpe Diem, Freedom, Vasiliki, Tranquility IV) priced where it is, targeting 20 to 25 percent. This is the growth, review, and referral engine. Do not raise these.
2. Reprice the Core fleet up (Mirracle to about $16,500, Gatsby to about $19,900) for 30 to 35 percent. These are recognizable and can hold the increase.
3. Reprice the Prestige fleet up materially (Sugaree to about $24,000, GTX 80 to about $26,500, flagships to $38,500 and $46,500) for 35 to 40 percent. They are premium, expensive vessels and are currently sold near cost.
4. Steer demand with the concierge. When a group is flexible, guide them to a Volume boat for the easy yes and the review, and reserve Prestige for buyers who want the flagship. Aim for roughly a 55 / 30 / 15 booking mix.
5. Put gift bags only on Prestige and the top experiences, where margin absorbs them.
6. Use the Volume fleet for paid acquisition and the founding client launch, since it can be both competitive and profitable, and it feeds the flywheel.

# Gale Broker Rates: Actuals and Cost-Up Repricing

Real Gale Yachting (galeyachting.com) public charter rates, read from each yacht page and entered into the Sandbox Yacht Broker Rates table. Boat Cost = Gale rate x 0.90. This replaces all prior boat cost estimates with actuals. Production pricing and production Airtable are not modified.

## What the Gale rate includes (from the Gale inclusions list)

Captain, stew, local idle fuel, basic beverages (water, sodas), ice, towels, Bluetooth audio, and water toys (floating mat, paddle board, snorkel gear). Therefore, for She Said Sail these are already inside Boat Cost and are zero incremental. The only costs She Said Sail adds on top are the produced layer: premium beverages and champagne, charcuterie, florals, styling and decor, gift bags, extra service labor, plus card fees and commission.

The retail prices were set by perceived tier, not by cost. The markup multiple ranges from 1.38x to 2.32x with no logic. The yachts that cost the most to source (Sugaree, GTX 80, Another One, Gatsby) carry the thinnest markups, so they are the least profitable, and some sell near cost.

## Minimum cost-up price to clear 30 percent (4hr Rosé)

Formula: minimum price = (Boat Cost + $2,450) / 0.645, where $2,450 is labor plus F&B and decor, and 0.645 = 1 minus the 30 percent margin minus the 5.5 percent fees. (F&B is an estimate, so these refine once the real F&B per tier is entered.)

The repricing is mostly upward, and largest on the high cost yachts (GTX 80, Sugaree, Gatsby). This is the opposite of cutting prices for penetration on those vessels. Penetration pricing only works on the genuinely cheap to source boats (Carpe Diem, Freedom, Vasiliki), which can be both competitive and profitable.

## Implications and recommendation

1. Reprice cost-up, per yacht, not by vibe. Use the minimum 30 percent price as the floor, then position above it.
2. Raise the high cost yachts materially. GTX 80 (new Pershing) and Sugaree (Bluegame) are premium, expensive vessels that are currently priced as mid. They should be among your most expensive offerings.
3. Make the cheap to source boats your volume and penetration engine. Carpe Diem, Freedom, and Vasiliki carry roughly 2.2x markups and can be the easy yes while staying profitable.
4. Cut the experience cost stack on entry tiers. The $1,200 gift bag is the difference between thin and unprofitable at the entry. Keep gift bags on Pink Palm and Monaco only.
5. Fix Another One and Carpe Diem Premium retail. The flagship 8hr cost is $22,500 and $18,000 respectively, so any list near $25,000 is at or below cost.
6. Get the last inputs: Compass Gale rate, plus 4hr and 6hr for Carpe Diem Premium and Another One.

## BRAND VOICE IN ONE LINE

Warm, composed, specific. Never trying too hard.

---

## WRITING RULES (HARD)

### Always
- Short sentences
- Sensory and scene-based language
- Emotional specificity over generic praise
- Grounded, certain tone
- Lead with the feeling, not the logistics
- "You" not "our guests"
- Active voice

### Never
- Em dashes (banned across all systems)
- Exclamation marks (zero)
- All-caps body copy
- Ellipsis for effect
- Parenthetical asides in hero copy
- "Don't hesitate to reach out"
- "We are excited to..."
- "VIP experience"
- "Unforgettable" / "Amazing" / "Incredible" / "Unbelievable"
- "Once in a lifetime"
- "Elite" / "Exclusive access" / "Luxury lifestyle"
- "Premium package" / "High-end" / "Epic" / "Next-level"
- "Book now!" energy (urgent or pushy)
- Multiple exclamation points

---

## SENTENCE STRUCTURE

- Ideal length: 8–15 words per sentence
- Break complex thoughts into two sentences
- Vary sentence length: short impact + longer supporting
- Paragraphs: 2–4 sentences max

---

## PAGE COPY HIERARCHY

### Hero
- Eyebrow: 3–6 word label. UPPERCASE, gold, tracked. Sets context.
- Headline: 4–10 words. Emotional. Present tense. Feeling first.
- Subheadline: 1–2 sentences. Expands the feeling. Grounds it in specifics.
- CTA: 2–5 words. Action verb. No urgency language.

### Section Labels (eyebrows)
- Short, uppercase, gold, tracked
- Examples: "WHAT'S INCLUDED" / "THE EXPERIENCE" / "HOW IT WORKS"

### Body Sections
- Lead with the experience, not the product
- Include sensory details: light, sound, movement, temperature
- Use scene-building: "As the sun drops..." / "Glasses raised..."
- Follow with trust and reassurance language
- Close sections with quiet confidence

### CTA Copy
Strong: "Reserve your date" / "Plan your escape" / "Start here"
Weak: "Book now!" / "Click here" / "Learn more" / "Submit"

---

## EMOTIONAL PACING

Pages should move through these emotional beats in order:
1. **Arrival** - The opening creates a mood. Not information.
2. **Desire** - The experience is described viscerally.
3. **Reassurance** - Trust signals. Everything is handled.
4. **Clarity** - What's included. No surprises.
5. **Action** - Clean, low-friction invitation to book.

---

## OCCASION LANGUAGE

Preferred phrasing:
- "birthday on the water"
- "bachelorette escape"
- "girls' trip"
- "group celebration"
- "your moment"

Avoid:
- "party boat"
- "wild night out"
- "epic bachelorette"
- "girl boss gang"

---

## TRUST + REASSURANCE COPY PATTERNS

Use patterns like:
- "Everything is already handled."
- "Your crew takes care of the rest."
- "No coordination required."
- "We take care of every detail."
- "All you do is show up."

These replace generic "We are the best" claims.

---

## SEO COPY RULES

- H1 must contain primary keyword naturally
- Meta description: 120–155 chars, emotion-first, includes keyword
- Alt text: descriptive scene, not "image of yacht" or "photo"
- No keyword stuffing
- Subheadings should read naturally, not like SEO tags

---

## CONCIERGE POSITIONING COPY

Pages should feel like a concierge wrote them, not a marketing team.

The concierge knows:
- What time the light is best
- What guests typically want
- What surprises work
- What not to say

The concierge does not:
- Oversell
- Repeat the same point twice
- Use templates that feel like templates
- Make the guest feel like a transaction

# SHE SAID SAIL — MASTER VISUAL DIRECTION
Version: 1.0 | Status: PRODUCTION | Owner: Will Hunt

---

## VISUAL IDENTITY SUMMARY

She Said Sail's visual language is warm luxury. Not loud. Not minimalist. Not corporate. It is cinematic, golden, calm, and emotionally alive.

---

## COLOR MOOD

The palette tells a story:
- **Navy** grounds and contains. Authority, calm, trust.
- **Gold** elevates and highlights. Warmth, celebration, quality.
- **Cream** softens and breathes. Editorial, editorial, rest.
- **White** clarifies. Clean. Hospitality.

Never use: neon, loud gradients, pastel pink, "millennial purple," overly saturated tones.

---

## PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

### What to Use
- Golden hour and soft evening light
- Champagne pours, raised glasses, relaxed toasts
- Groups looking at each other, not at camera
- Water reflections, sail movement, natural horizon
- Candid hosting moments: crew attending to detail
- Women laughing, comfortable, proud
- Tablescapes with subtle luxury details
- Dusk light: the sky doing its work

### What to Avoid
- Posed "luxury" with forced smiles
- Men dominating the frame
- Party chaos, messy scenes
- Nightclub aesthetics
- Bright daylight with blown-out skies
- Stock photography (people who look like stock photos)
- Screaming, jumping, hands-in-the-air energy

### Composition
- Prefer wide editorial shots over close-up product shots
- Human presence in every hero image
- Vessel as context, not as subject
- Horizon lines: slightly below center
- Warm color grade, not cold or desaturated

## SECTION VISUAL PATTERNS

### Hero
- Full bleed image
- Dark overlay (navy, 35–50% opacity)
- White headline text
- Single gold CTA button

### Content Sections (light background)
- Cream or white background
- Navy text
- Gold accents on labels and dividers
- Images: warm-toned, editorial

### Feature/Trust Sections
- Navy background with white text and gold accents
- OR cream background with navy text
- Clean, uncluttered layout

### Card Components
- White card on cream background
- Subtle shadow
- Gold rule or icon accent
- Clean typography hierarchy

---

## ICON STYLE

- Thin line icons (1.5–2px stroke)
- Gold (#c9a84c) on light backgrounds
- White on dark backgrounds
- No filled/solid icons
- No emoji in UI (except email HTML)
- Size: 20–24px in content, 16px in compact lists

---

## LAYOUT PHILOSOPHY

- Generosity of space over information density
- Wide margins breathe luxury
- Content should never feel squeezed
- One visual focal point per section
- No more than 3 columns on desktop for content sections
- 2 columns maximum for feature cards

---

## ANIMATION DIRECTION

- Subtle fade-in on scroll (0.4s, opacity 0 to 1)
- No bounce, no spring, no overshoot
- Parallax: very subtle if used (5–10% range only)
- No moving elements that distract from reading
- Mobile: no scroll animations (performance + distraction)

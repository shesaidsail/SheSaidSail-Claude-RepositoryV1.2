## VISUAL PHILOSOPHY

She Said Sail does not look like a charter company website.

It looks like a lifestyle hospitality brand with editorial restraint. The visual language is closer to a boutique travel magazine than a boat rental marketplace.

---

## PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

### What We Show
- The guests, not the yacht (people feel first)
- Candid moments over posed content
- Golden hour light when possible
- Water, sky, and atmosphere as supporting characters
- Details: champagne glasses, deck views, group laughter
- Real women having a real experience

### What We Never Show
- Stock photography
- Fake-looking luxury imagery
- Men in suits at sea (that is Mare Executive)
- Celebrities or famous faces
- Overly photoshopped, over-processed imagery
- Boats in a marina (active water preferred)
- Empty decks (always with people)

### Image Mood
- Warm (golden light, not cold blue tones)
- Soft (not harsh HDR contrast)
- Intimate (medium shots and close-ups over wide panoramas)
- Social (showing connection between people)
- Effortless (natural, unposed where possible)

## COLOR USAGE RULES

### Navy (#0a2342)
Used for: hero overlays, section backgrounds (alternating), footer, headings on cream
Never use as: full-page background for long copy sections

### Gold (#c9a84c)
Used for: eyebrow labels, CTA buttons, divider lines, hover states, price display
Never use as: large background fills or body copy color

### Cream (#f4f1ec)
Used for: alternating section backgrounds, form backgrounds, card backgrounds
This is the warm alternative to pure white. Prefer cream over white for section fills.

### White (#ffffff)
Used for: text on dark backgrounds, card interiors, form fields
Keep it clean. No off-whites mixing with the cream.

---

## LAYOUT PRINCIPLES

1. Every section should have clear visual breathing room.
2. Do not stack dense copy sections back to back. Alternate with image, stat, or quote sections.
3. The eye should flow naturally down the page without effort.
4. Left-align body copy. Center only for short eyebrow labels or one-line headlines.
5. Full-bleed images break the layout rhythm in a good way. Use them intentionally.

---

## WHAT LUXURY LOOKS LIKE HERE

- Generous padding (not cramped)
- One accent color used sparingly
- Photography that makes you feel something
- Typography that is confident without trying hard
- White space that feels like silence, not emptiness
- Details that are precise and considered

---

## WHAT LUXURY DOES NOT LOOK LIKE HERE

- Ornate borders and decorative frames
- Heavy use of gradients
- Glowing buttons
- Complex animations on scroll
- Gold foil textures
- Marble backgrounds
- "LUXURY" written anywhere on the page

---

## ICON AND ILLUSTRATION STANDARDS

- Use line icons only (no filled icons unless system UI context requires it)
- Icon weight: thin to medium (1.5px to 2px stroke)
- Color: gold (#c9a84c) or navy (#0a2342)
- Size: 24px default, 32px for hero context
- No emoji in page copy
- No clipart or vector illustrations that feel generic

---

## VIDEO DIRECTION (if applicable)

- Autoplay: yes, but muted and loop
- Sound: never on autoplay
- Length: 10 to 30 seconds looping
- Mood: slow, editorial
- Never: action sports energy, fast cuts, DJ music
- Always: a fallback image for non-video environments

---

## PATTERN RECOGNITION

A She Said Sail page should be instantly recognizable by:
1. The navy + gold palette used with restraint
2. Georgia serif typography with clean sans accents
3. Warm cream backgrounds alternating with navy
4. Photography with golden hour emotional warmth
5. No noise, no clutter, no visual competition
